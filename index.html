Fatal Pedestrian & Cyclist Crash Risk Factors in Oregon

A Spatial Analysis, 2015–2024

Fatal pedestrian and cyclist crashes are not spread evenly across Oregon. This project links a decade of NHTSA FARS fatal-crash records to Census tract–level demographic and commuting data to map where these crashes cluster and which roadway, environmental, and community characteristics tend to accompany them. A reproducible pipeline builds a 1,049-record victim-level dataset; DBSCAN spatial clustering surfaces 11 systemic hotspots, which skew heavily toward arterial roads and lack safety infrastructure that other locations statewide have more of. The goal is descriptive, not predictive: giving transportation agencies a shortlist of corridors and communities worth closer investigation.

Owner team: Simon Thompson & Rohan Srinivasa Babu — Peer Stakeholder POs: Aiyana Brown, Amaya Supancich-McCord, Seira Ramchandani
